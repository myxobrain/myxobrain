--- R02 · MYXO PAPER №02 · THE BLOB
Gambar: research/02-the-blob.png
Sumber: Nakagaki, Yamada & Tóth · Nature · 2000

Physarum polycephalum has no neurons at all.

It's one giant cell with many nuclei, spreading as a network of tubes.

In 2000 it solved a maze.

--- R03 · MYXO PAPER №03 · THE MAZE
Gambar: research/03-the-maze.png
Sumber: Nakagaki, Yamada & Tóth · Nature · 2000

Nakagaki filled a maze with slime mold, then put oat flakes at the entrance and the exit.

Hours later only one tube was left: the shortest path between the two.

No map. No plan. Just flow.

--- R04 · MYXO PAPER №04 · TOKYO
Gambar: research/04-tokyo.png
Sumber: Tero et al. · Science · 2010

2010: researchers placed oat flakes in the pattern of the cities around Tokyo and let a slime mold grow.

The network it built was comparable to the real rail system in efficiency and resilience.

Engineers took years. The blob took about a day.

--- R05 · MYXO PAPER №05 · THE RULE
Gambar: research/05-the-rule.png
Sumber: Tero, Kobayashi & Nakagaki · J. Theor. Biol. · 2007

The whole trick fits in one line:

dD/dt = f(|Q|) − γD

A tube that carries flow thickens. A tube that doesn't, fades.

That's the rule MYXOBRAIN's Physarum runs every hour.

1986: White, Southgate, Thomson and Brenner publish the complete wiring diagram of a nervous system.

Your brain: ~86 billion neurons.

--- R09 · MYXO PAPER №09 · THE REMAP

33 years after the first map, Cook and colleagues mapped the whole worm again, both sexes, every chemical synapse and gap junction.

That 2019 connectome is the wiring MYXOBRAIN's worm runs on, and the ring you see on the site.

--- R10 · MYXO PAPER №10 · THE SPIKE
Gambar: research/10-the-spike.png
Sumber: Shiu et al. · Nature · 2024

Every neuron in the worm is a leaky integrate-and-fire cell: charge up, leak, spike, reset.

The constants come from a 2024 whole-brain model of the fruit fly, not from a worm paper. The site says that out loud.

--- R12 · MYXO PAPER №12 · GLASS FISH
Gambar: research/12-glass-fish.png
Sumber: Ahrens et al. · Nature Methods · 2013

A zebrafish larva is a few millimetres long and almost see-through.

In 2013, light-sheet microscopy recorded nearly every neuron in its brain at the same time.

One of the very few brains we can watch whole.

--- R13 · MYXO PAPER №13 · THE GLOW
Gambar: research/13-the-glow.png
Sumber: Chen et al. · Nature · 2013

How do you see a neuron think?

GCaMP6, a protein that glows when calcium rushes in during a spike.

MYXOBRAIN's fry simulates that sensor too: its rise, its decay, its ceiling.

--- R14 · MYXO PAPER №14 · FIRE TOGETHER
Gambar: research/14-fire-together.png
Sumber: Hebb · The Organization of Behavior · 1949

Donald Hebb, 1949: when one cell repeatedly helps fire another, the connection between them grows.

Later summed up as "fire together, wire together."

It's how the fry's synapses learn in MYXOBRAIN.

--- R15 · MYXO PAPER №15 · THE CLOCK
Gambar: research/15-the-clock.png
Sumber: Grama & Engert · Front. Neural Circuits · 2012

21 ± 7 milliseconds.

That's how long a larval zebrafish tectal neuron holds on to its input before it leaks away.

Small numbers like this set how the fry in MYXOBRAIN reacts.

--- R17 · MYXO PAPER №17 · THE BRIDGE
Gambar: research/17-the-bridge.png
Sumber: Goss et al. · Naturwissenschaften · 1989

Give an ant colony two bridges to food, one short, one long.

Ants on the short bridge come back sooner, so its pheromone builds faster, so more ants take it.

No leader. No map. The colony still picks right.

--- R18 · MYXO PAPER №18 · EVAPORATION
Gambar: research/18-evaporation.png
Sumber: Dorigo & Gambardella · IEEE Trans. Evol. Comput. · 1997

If pheromone never faded, a colony would follow its first good idea forever.

Evaporation lets old trails die so new ones can win.

The same knob lives in MYXOBRAIN's ant: rho.

--- R19 · MYXO PAPER №19 · THE SALESMAN
Gambar: research/19-the-salesman.png
Sumber: Dorigo & Gambardella · 1997

The travelling salesman problem: visit every city once, as short as possible.

In 1997 Dorigo and Gambardella threw virtual ants at it: lay pheromone, let it evaporate, choose by chance.

It worked well enough to start a field.

--- R20 · MYXO PAPER №20 · THE LIMITS
Gambar: research/20-the-limits.png
Sumber: Stützle & Hoos · Future Gener. Comput. Syst. · 2000

One fix for ants that get stuck: clamp the pheromone.

MAX–MIN Ant System sets a floor so no trail disappears and a ceiling so none takes over for good.

MYXOBRAIN's ant uses the same bounds.

In MYXOBRAIN each asset is a food source for the colony.

--- R22 · MYXO PAPER №22 · THE SWARM
Gambar: research/22-the-swarm.png
Sumber: Seeley · Honeybee Democracy · 2010

When a honeybee colony splits, about ten thousand bees hang on a branch.

A few hundred scouts fly out to look for a new home.

What happens next is one of the best-studied group decisions in biology.

--- R23 · MYXO PAPER №23 · THE DANCE
Gambar: research/23-the-dance.png
Sumber: Seeley & Buhrman · Behav. Ecol. Sociobiol. · 1999

A scout that finds a good cavity dances for it. A great cavity gets a longer, more vigorous dance.

More dancing recruits more scouts, who dance too.

No bee compares all the options. The swarm does.

--- R24 · MYXO PAPER №24 · THE QUORUM
Gambar: research/24-the-quorum.png
Sumber: Passino & Seeley · Behav. Ecol. Sociobiol. · 2006

The swarm doesn't wait for everyone to agree.

When enough scouts are present at one site at the same time, a quorum, the swarm takes off.

MYXOBRAIN's bee moves its capital the same way.

--- R25 · MYXO PAPER №25 · SPEED VS ACCURACY
Gambar: research/25-speed-vs-accuracy.png
Sumber: Passino & Seeley · 2006

Set the quorum low and the swarm decides fast but sometimes picks a worse home.

Set it high and it chooses better but slower.

Every trader knows this trade-off. The bees tuned it first.

In MYXOBRAIN the 11 assets are 11 candidate nests.

--- R27 · MYXO PAPER №27 · RUN AND TUMBLE
Gambar: research/27-run-and-tumble.png
Sumber: Berg & Brown · Nature · 1972

E. coli moves in two modes: run, a straight swim, and tumble, a quick spin to a random new direction.

That's all it can do. It's enough to find food.

--- R28 · MYXO PAPER №28 · THE TRACKER
Gambar: research/28-the-tracker.png
Sumber: Berg & Brown · 1972

To see run and tumble, Howard Berg built a microscope that tracks a single bacterium in 3D as it swims.

The tracks showed runs getting longer when the cell swam toward food.

--- R29 · MYXO PAPER №29 · THE MEMORY
Gambar: research/29-the-memory.png
Sumber: Macnab & Koshland · PNAS · 1972

A bacterium doesn't measure where the food is.

It compares how much food it senses now with a few seconds ago. Better: keep running. Worse: tumble.

A memory of seconds, and it works.

--- R30 · MYXO PAPER №30 · TOO SMALL
Gambar: research/30-too-small.png
Sumber: Berg & Purcell · Biophys. J. · 1977

Why compare time and not space?

Berg and Purcell worked it out: a cell a couple of microns long can't reliably tell its front from its back by smell.

So it remembers instead.
