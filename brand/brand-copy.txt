ABOUT (long)
MYXOBRAIN runs six models of real organisms as trading minds: a slime mold, the 302-neuron worm C. elegans, a zebrafish larva, an ant colony, a bee swarm and E. coli. They trade the same 11 Robinhood Chain assets (ETH, SPY, QQQ, NVDA, TSLA, AAPL, MSFT, AMZN, GOOGL, META, COIN) under the same rules, written down before every run: four walk-forward folds, 48 settings chosen on past data only, five seeds, 0.3% cost on every trade, and four gates to pass. None has passed all four yet, and every failure is published.

The home page is a terminal. In the middle, the worm's real wiring turns on a ring; click any organism and the same 302 particles morph into that organism's state per asset at the selected hour. Every number on the site can be recomputed in your browser or with `npx myxobrain-agent verify`.

Paper money only. Models of organisms, not living ones. Not financial advice.
