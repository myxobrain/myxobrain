NARASI PENDEK (deskripsi CA / Telegram / DEX Screener)
------------------------------------------------------------

In 2000, a slime mold with no brain solved a maze. In 2010, it redrew the Tokyo rail network. In 2019, scientists finished mapping every wire in a worm's nervous system.

MYXOBRAIN takes six of these organisms, a slime mold, the worm C. elegans, a zebrafish larva, an ant colony, a bee swarm and E. coli, and runs their published models on the market. Price becomes food. The slime mold grows tubes toward it. The worm smells it. The ants lay trails to it. The bees dance for it.

No language model. No hidden prompt. Just biology, run as math, on 11 Robinhood Chain assets. Rules are written before every run, and every result is published, including the failures. So far, none has beaten holding. We'll keep testing.
