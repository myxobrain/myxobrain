Studio

Owner only. The key is in studio/data/burn/secrets.json on the server, or STUDIO_KEY in studio/.env.

01 · Token

The CA shows on myxobrain.com (header and the burn ledger) with a copy button and a Buy on Pons link within a minute of saving. Leave it empty to hide it.

02 · Buyback wallet

Use a fresh wallet that only holds the ETH for buybacks. The key is encrypted on the server and never shown again. Never paste your main wallet.

03 · Buyback and burn

Enter dollars. The panel pays the matching ETH at the live price, buys the token (Pons curve before graduation, Uniswap v4 after), then sends exactly what it bought to the dead address. Two real transactions.

04 · Burn supply

Burns tokens the burn wallet already holds, with no buy. Send the supply you want to burn to the burn wallet first, then enter a dollar amount or press Max to burn all of it. One real transaction.

05 · History
